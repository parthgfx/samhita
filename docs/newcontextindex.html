Our Story

This is the ladder
to an irreversible
increase in income

Skilling & Jobs
Credit & Market Linkages
Healthcare & Social Security

This is the ladder to an irreversible increase in income. It's made up of skilling, jobs, market linkages, credit, healthcare, and social security.

If savings are spent on a health emergency, that's a missing rung. If staying afloat means borrowing informally at predatory rates that consume earnings, that's another broken rung. If a business is growing but a machine breaks, and there's no credit to repair it, that's another broken rung.

This ladder cannot be climbed. And fixing one rung doesn't help. We need to build a strong ladder — one that holds at every step.

As a Prosperity Accelerator, Samhita brings together those who build each rung: CSR, philanthropy, financial institutions, governments, and NGOs. This ensures our citizens receive a continuum of scholarships, skills, jobs, affordable credit, market linkages, and government benefits.

This is how we build a citizen-centric livelihood network so all of India can climb.
