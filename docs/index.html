Three ways a livelihood gets built

Who

Runs a home-based sweet-making business

Outcome

147% increase in annual income

  1. 01Credit guarantee–backed loan unlocks working capital
  2. 02Catering course launches her sweet-making venture
  3. 03Invests in equipment, packaging and government certification
  4. 04Health insurance, pension and children's scholarships secured
  5. 05Builds monthly savings, invests for the long term
  6. 06Onboarded to e-commerce, runs festival sales campaigns
  7. 07Hires two local women, expands production
Who

Dairy and crop farmer, tied to informal credit

Outcome

158% increase in annual income

  1. 01Returnable grant funds better cattle management
  2. 02Cattle management training, linked to govt vet camps
  3. 03Family enrolled in government health insurance
  4. 04Farmer Collective (FPO) negotiates bulk fertilizer deals and builds a milk chilling unit
  5. 05Hires a worker for cattle and farm operations
  6. 06Credit-backed loan launches a dairy products business
  7. 07Sells to larger dairy brands through digital markets
Who

Aspiring beautician, first in her family to work

Outcome

Steady, consistently growing income

  1. 01Enrolls in beautician course with financial support
  2. 02Completes internship linked to her training
  3. 03Resume-building and interview prep completed
  4. 04Secures a job at a salon in Mumbai
  5. 05Returnable grant covers travel and settling costs
  6. 06Begins saving regularly, builds long-term investments
  7. 07Pursues management diploma, promoted to Assistant Manager
