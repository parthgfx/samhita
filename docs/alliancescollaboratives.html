How a coalition gets built

What we do

  1. Test innovation and scale what works.

    Your capital funds the hard early stage: pilots, demonstrations and the systems other partners can later use and fund. This gives the alliance its first real asset, proof that the idea works, before anyone else takes a risk on it.

  2. We design tools that make your money go further.

    Things like guarantee funds and credit-readiness systems are built around your funding, so banks and lenders feel safe giving loans at a much bigger scale.

  3. Build a pipeline government and industry can plug into.

    Once your pilot proves the idea, we build the awareness campaigns, paperwork and on-ground teams needed so government schemes and CSR budgets can start co-funding the same people your money first reached.

  4. We manage the structure so your vision stays intact.

    You decide the goal, the funding and the risk you're willing to take. We set up the rules, roles and accountability so other partners can join in without changing what you set out to do.

  5. We track outcomes, not just outputs.

    For example: a guarantee fund of about $26K unlocked about $520K in loans — 20 times the original amount. A pilot that unlocked $52–$210 in CSR funding per person is a 6x return. This is what we report back to you.

  1. You plug into a model that has been tested.

    By the time you come in, the anchor partner has already funded the pilot and taken on the early risk. Your money enters an alliance with a proven model.

  2. Your funding pools with money that's already flowing.

    CSR funding of $52–$210 per person works alongside government support of $10 to $31,500+ per person, already unlocked through the same coalition. Your money isn't working alone, it's working next to public and institutional money.

  3. We show you a clear return, not just reach.

    Training, digital skills and business support funded by program partners create a 6x return on the original funding.

  4. You stay inside one accountable system.

    Shared reporting and measurement mean you can see how the whole collaborative is performing.

  5. We ensure efficiency.

    Because roles and structure are set up early, your funding adds something new to the alliance instead of overlapping with what someone else already pays for. Nothing gets duplicated, and nothing gets wasted.

Holding the centre

Why Samhita

A collaborative succeeds when somebody can do all three: convene, execute and hold the centre. Samhita has experience doing this across funders, companies, government, NGOs, banks and technology partners.

What keeps the ladder standing

What makes our alliances work

We take an alliance from anchor-funder engagement through to scaling and sustainability planning, so funders get one accountable partner across design, delivery and evaluation, instead of separate agencies for each.

  • A modest anchor commitment consistently unlocks several times its value. REVIVE turned $3.1 million in secretariat capital into $18 million in total funding. The WASH Platform delivered a 7.5x return on the Gates Foundation's secretariat support.
  • Every alliance launches with a defined governance body, a dedicated PMU, and documented CSR, FCRA and CSR-1 compliance in place from day one. Where an alliance combines foreign and domestic funding, fund-flow structures are confirmed with each partner's legal and compliance team before launch, in line with FCRA fund-transfer requirements.
  • 250+ corporates and donors, 20+ financial institutions, and 350+ social-sector partners means a new alliance starts on existing trust rather than a cold start.
  • Fifteen years, five flagship alliances reaching over 1.7 crore lives, and now the Livelihood Acceleration Network, a national platform already reaching over a million people, built from what those alliances proved worked.
