Rung 05 · the sequences

Three ways a livelihood gets built

Pick a person. Follow the sequence.

Who

Runs a shop, a kitchen, a tailoring unit

Outcome

+38% median monthly income at 12 months

  1. 01Identified through a local partner and verified in person
  2. 02₹15,000–40,000 returnable grant released in tranches
  3. 03Business basics: pricing, digital payments, inventory
  4. 04Repayment recycles into the next three entrepreneurs
Who

Rides, delivers, drives, cleans on demand

Outcome

2.1× improvement in income stability

  1. 01Onboarded through platform and aggregator partnerships
  2. 02Asset finance for a vehicle, phone or equipment
  3. 03Insurance, savings nudges and grievance support
  4. 04Portable earnings record they actually own
Who

Entering paid work, often against the odds

Outcome

71% still employed after one year

  1. 01Household conversation before enrolment — consent matters
  2. 02Sector-matched skilling with childcare and travel covered
  3. 03Placement with employers audited for safe workplaces
  4. 04Six-month retention follow-up, not a placement photo
