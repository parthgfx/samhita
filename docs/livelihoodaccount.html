Livelihood Account ·

Years of training, work and repayment should not be erased by a change of city, job or lender.

SkillsJobsCreditMarketsSchemes

Explore a Livelihood Account pilot →

Every day, people in India work, learn, sell and repay loans. Proof of all this already exists in training centres, employers, banks, government schemes and women's groups, but the person can't use it.

The Livelihood Account lets them bring that proof together, with their consent, to get their next job, loan, market or scheme.

01The proof exists. It doesn't travel.

The problem

Imagine you've been a good driver for ten years. You apply for a new job, and the new employer has never heard of you. You can't prove your ten years, so you start again from zero.

This happens to millions of people every day.

Rich in history. Poor in proof.

Every employer, lender and buyer asks the same question: can I trust this person's record? Today the proof exists, but it can't travel with the person. The Livelihood Account is the layer that lets it.

02

The idea

The Livelihood Account creates no central database. Records stay where they already are. The account is a key the person holds, which lets them open those records and show them to someone they choose.

How it works

  1. 01

    The proof already exists.

    It's held by training centres, employers, banks, women's groups and government schemes.

  2. 02

    The person decides who sees it.

    Nothing is shared without their yes. They choose what, with whom, and for what.

  3. 03

    Every record is verified.

    Every record is checked at its source, as a tokenised, verifiable credential, and can't be faked.

Safeguards built in

  • Consent every time.

    Asked each time, and it can be withdrawn.

  • Data stays at source.

    No central database, and no copies.

  • Verifiable credentials.

    Checked at source, so they can't be faked.

  • Data protection by design.

    Built in line with India's DPDP Act.

  • Built on India's DPI.

    Uses existing digital public infrastructure.

  • Works for everyone.

    On a phone, or with help from a local facilitator.

03Every opportunity builds the next

Ravi's story (illustrative)

  1. Training.

    Ravi finishes a welding course at a training institute in Hubli.

  2. Job.

    With his yes, the institute's record reaches an employer, and he is hired.

  3. Record.

    His wages are paid digitally and become part of his record.

  4. Loan.

    A lender sees a year of steady income and funds his welding kit.

  5. Market.

    He takes on his own jobs, and his record wins him a regular client.

  6. Growth.

    His next loan is bigger.
