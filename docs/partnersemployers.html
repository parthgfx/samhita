For employers · Your rung: talent for hard-to-fill roles

We identify candidates through trusted local networks, train against actual role requirements, support assessment and placement, and stay engaged during the transition into work.

What employers can use us for

  • Role and demand mapping.

  • Candidate sourcing through local networks.

  • Technical and employability training.

  • Assessment, certification and interview readiness.

  • Placement coordination and documentation.

  • 90-day retention support and worker feedback.

  • Women-focused recruitment and workplace-readiness pathways.

  • Green jobs and emerging-role pipelines.

Why this works

Employer demand shapes the training from the start. That improves relevance for the employer and gives candidates a clearer route from learning to earning.
