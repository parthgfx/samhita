About us · Who we are

Building the ladder that helps people earn, progress and prosper.

We believe economic mobility shouldn't depend on finding the right rung by chance.

So that progress compounds instead of resetting with every new institution they meet.

People should be able to climb from:

  • Skills → work
  • Informal finance → formal finance
  • A first market → a growing business

01How we build the ladder

What makes our model different

We design backwards from the change all of us want to see - income, jobs, revenue, resilience, access.

  • Strategy, program design, partner selection and capacity building, program management, technology, measurement and communications are all aligned to your institution's priorities and stakeholders.
  • We connect services across time instead of treating each program as a fresh start for the same person.
  • We use grant capital strategically to unlock credit, public schemes, markets and additional funding to maximize your impact.
  • Government, banks, employers, platforms, funders and local organisations each do what they do best; we build the connective execution layer between them.
  • We scale evidence backed initiatives and measure continuously to constantly improve outcomes

02Two structures. One ladder.

Samhita and Collective Good Foundation — our structure

We work under a shared brand, with one strategy, delivered through two different organisations.

Public charitable trust

Collective Good Foundation (CGF)

  • Registered under the Registration Act, 1908.
  • Receives CSR funding, philanthropic donations, and government and multilateral grants.

Social enterprise

Samhita Social Ventures (SSV)

  • Incorporated as a private limited company.
  • Takes on consulting service contracts with banks, corporates, CSR and other private-sector partners.

Why two organisations?

Each funding route has different legal requirements, and a single-entity structure can't maximize impact and collaboration. Two structures let us partner responsibly with everyone from a government department to a bank to a CSR client to a family foundation.
